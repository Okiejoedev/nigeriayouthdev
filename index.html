Who We Are

Shaping the Future of Nigerian Youth

The Nigeria Youth Society Development is dedicated to creating sustainable pathways for youth empowerment across the nation. We believe that investing in young people is the most effective way to build a prosperous Nigeria.

Based in Abuja, we work across all six geopolitical zones, partnering with communities, government agencies, and international organizations to deliver programs that create real, lasting impact.

What We Do

Our Programs

We run targeted initiatives designed to equip young Nigerians with the tools, knowledge, and networks they need to thrive.

Education & Scholarships

Providing access to quality education through scholarship programs, mentorship, and academic support for underserved youth.

Vocational Skills Training

Equipping young people with practical skills in technology, agriculture, crafts, and entrepreneurship for self-reliance.

Community Development

Engaging youth in community service, civic responsibility, and grassroots development projects across Nigeria.

Leadership & Advocacy

Training the next generation of leaders through workshops, conferences, and policy engagement opportunities.

Health & Wellness

Promoting physical and mental well-being through awareness campaigns, sports, and health outreach programs.

Digital Literacy

Bridging the digital divide by teaching computer skills, coding, and safe internet practices to young Nigerians.
